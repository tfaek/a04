Tali’s mouth was dry, her hands unconsciously pressed against Jane. Looking up into her eyes, she feared she’d melt right through her suit. Looping her fingers around Jane’s neck, she pulled her down on top of herself. Playfulness bloomed into sincerity, and she pressed her mask to Jane’s forehead.

“So do I.” She breathed, holding Jane in her shining gaze. “More than anything.”

Every fibre of Tali’s being wished for the glass between them to vanish. As if reading her mind, Jane took Tali’s hand from her own neck. She planted a long, soft kiss on Tali’s skin.

She wanted it to last forever, to take Jane’s affection and bind it to her very soul. Those lips pecked along her naked, tingling surface. Countless nights she’d dreamed of it, longed to know what such impossibility might be like.

Now they were here, sharing the impossible together. And it was better than she’d ever imagined.

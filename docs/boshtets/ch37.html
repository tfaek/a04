Chapter 37

Jane cursed and fired three shots in quick succession as Tali dragged her further. They broke the shields of one of the mercenaries, but then the third one didn't have enough power left behind it to break through his armor, merely giving the Batarian a mean kick in the chestplate. At least he stopped coming after them and hastily took cover.

Their escape had gone to shit almost immediately after they'd crawled out of the sewage. The Blue Suns had evidently noticed her being gone and either quickly deduced where they'd go, or simply send out enough men to get lucky. Either way, the bastards had beset them as they had still been collecting themselves, Tali talking to Garrus over the radio and Shepard trying to find a way to stand stable with her wounded leg. Jane suspected the only reason she wasn't dead yet was that the mercenaries were trying to capture her rather than kill. Tali had no such luxury, but her shields held up easily against the mediocrely aimed fire of the four guys chasing them – in fact, they probably could've made a stand and cut them down relatively quickly.

But there was no time. There was no way of knowing how many more pursuers would be able to join the hunt, or when they would get there. So the two of them retreated through the undergrowth, firing off quick series of shots to keep the enemy at bay. They were moving far too slow for Jane's liking, but she still couldn't walk properly; in fact, she could barely stand.

Off to the half right, in some distance, she could see flashes of white and blue behind the foliage. She frowned; there they were, as she had feared. Moments later, someone opened up with an assault rifle the sound of which Jane was very familiar with.

At least Garrus is close.

Tali fired her shotgun to Shepard's left, and Shepard turned to snap off another round of shots until she was interrupted by her quarian friend pulling her hard.

"Quick Jane! We can't go around!"

Even though she knew not what Tali was talking about, Jane gritted her teeth and tried to ignore the pain as she forced her leg to perform well above what it was fit for right now. A moment later, a branch snapped into her face, giving her a painful scratch on the forehead, and they broke free onto a clearing.

Goddammit!

As much as she tried, her leg was giving out quickly. It took everything she had just to not lose speed even faster. And the clearing was wide.

We won't make it like this!

The situation felt familiar in the worst way. But this would not happen again. She wouldn't let it. And so she ignored the rising panic and quickly yet methodically scanned the area for a good way forward or at least some cover, until she found both in the form of a dry streambed to the half left that offered the only protection to be seen. She pulled on Tali and shouted over the noise of the fighting; somehow, Tali understood and together they made good speed towards the protection of the ditch. Dirt and small stones were flying around everywhere when just as they reached it, their pursuers broke through the treeline behind them. Tali and Jane jumped inside.

Are they actually trying to kill me now? , she thought – and then she thought nothing for a second as the impact made the pain in her leg explode with terrible intensity. She fell on all fours and groaned.

Jane hated leg wounds.

Tali's shotgun barked, and the sound helped her to push through. Teeth gritted, she threw herself onto the bank and opened fire. One Blue Sun was lying in the grass already; Tali's signature combination of an Overload and a doubled up shotgun blast, she reckoned. Her pistol in Shepard's hands had not quite the same punch, but was still a Spectre grade weapon, and so a quick succession of five shots broke through the shields of the Batarian she was gunning for and killed him with the last one, popping his head like a melon.

Two and a half seconds had passed since they had jumped into the ditch. There were only three enemies left immediately in front of them. A grim smile spread on her lips.

It turned into a snarl a moment later when she recognized one of the men as the Salarian, who as soon as he had closed in to about twenty meters unleashed a wave of biotic energy that forced them both into cover. When they came up again, Tali was greeted with a furious fusillade that made her shields light up like a christmas tree, and Jane saw another biotic warp come straight at her.

She threw herself to the right; as she fell, the world appeared to slow down around her, as it sometimes did in combat. She could see Tali perform an Overload and then hectically bring up her gun. Too hectic. She just knew that Tali wouldn't land a good hit. As Jane's right buttcheek impacted the dirt, she began to aim; before she could fire she began to roll over, pushed by the kinetic force of the tiny biotic explosion unleashed where she had just been. By the time her gun was on target, she was upside-down. It was an impossible shot.

She nailed it anyway, and the Turian she'd been aiming for died before his body hit the ground. Then she landed on her left leg and the pain made her almost black out. But Jane was furious, and her rage carried her through it as she pushed herself off the ground and turned to face their enemies, only to barely dodge the butt of an assault rifle. Instinctively, she moved in and wrenched the thing out of the assailants hands. She was met with the ugly grimace of a Batarian rushing at her. They fell backwards onto the other bank; the impact pushed the air out of Jane's lungs. Jane somehow managed to evade the wild haymaker the big alien sitting on top of her threw at her face and it connected with the grass next to her left ear with a loud thud. It was the kind of miss that would have broken the wrist of anyone who wasn't a Turian or Krogan – but the Blue Sun was clad in a full combat suit. It made the big bastard even heavier than he already was and it's edges painfully pressed into her groin and lower abdomen. Her own hands were naked, so if she punched the squint, she'd break her fingers. In the background, she could hear the repeated crack of a very familiar sniper rifle.

Another punch came in and she barely stopped it with both hands. Immediately, the Batarian's other fist slammed into Jane's ribs and she coughed.

I'm losing!

In the corner of her eye, there was frantic movement. Tali was fighting the Salarian.

Jane snarled and rammed her knees- both of them – into the alien's ass as hard as she could. The pain was indescribable, but she succeeded in throwing the man off her hips and the Batarian fell forward, catching himself with one arm. The other came down as a fist, but it was a poorly aimed punch with no force behind it. Jane easily caught it with her left.

With her right, she pulled the mercenary's knife from the sheath at his upper left leg and buried it in between his chestplate's collar and the lowe edge of his helmet. Then she slipped it out just as the shocked mercenary pulled back and clutched his neck. Jane used the opening to stab him twice more, this time into his left armpit. Warm blood was running down her hand when she finally dropped the weapon to try and push off the dying Batarian who had gone limp by now and fallen forward. Past the stinking head of the mercenary, Jane could see Tali still fighting the Salarian. None of them had a firearm, but Tali was wielding her knife. She sliced and kicked like a maddened fury, but the Salarian was even faster. Jane could see Tali's left arm hanging a little, and in between her attacks, she was hit by two quick jabs in the short time it took Jane to free herself of the dying man on top of her.

She looked down for a moment to pull out her right leg; just as she did so, there was a yell and a hiss, and when she looked back up, it was just in time to see Tali get hit by the dark energies of a biotic blast. She was thrown through the air with a shriek and hit the ground hard a meter to Shepard's right, her helmet producing a distinct, ugly crack as her neck snapped back and her head impacted a stone.

The Salarian had retrieved his weapon and stood there with such a disgustingly smug attitude that Jane could think about little else than to tear his face off. On his cheek there was a deep, bleeding gash. Tali's knife lay at his feet.

"Close combat adequate, Mrs. Zorah. It was my pleasure."

He lowered the gun, and the smirk on his features transformed into the cold stare of a seasoned killer. As the weapon came close to pointing at Tali's head, Jane's world slowed down again. She could see the situation - how it would play out and what she could do about it - with complete clarity and with what felt like all the time in the world to make her move.

After what had, in truth, been perhaps the tenth of a second, Jane acted. It was an easy decision. Ignoring the protests of her left leg one last time, she put every ounce of strength she had left into a desperate lunge.

Next thing she knew, she was lying on her side in the dirt. With her last strength, she rolled herself on her back so she would be able to see something. The Salarian just stood there, looking genuinely surprised for the first time since Shepard had known him. Garrus was still shooting somewhere close.

At least I wiped the stupid grin off his face for a moment.

Tali came into view now; she knelt at Shepard's side, touching her frantically, eyes wide behind her mask. She said things, but Jane barely heard anything. The Salarian was still standing there, but he was no longer looking down at them. For some reason, this intrigued Jane enough to keep watching the man, even though the pain in her chest that was now setting in was making it difficult to focus on anything, and Tali taking up half her field of view didn't help.

The Salarian's eyes widened and he snapped into action, forming a barrier just in time to not get torn into pieces by a savage warp slamming into him. Before he could recover, his shields came under fire from an automatic weapon. He returned the fire with his pistol, but only shortly. Then he had to fend off another biotic attack. A moment later, a black and white blur appeared from the part of Shepard's vision that was taken up by Tali and came down on the Salarian with a vengeance. He performed a spinning kick of impressive speed that sent the woman's – it was a human woman – gun, it looked like some type of submachinegun, flying, but payed for it with a punch taken to the chin; if he hadn't taken it there, it would've crushed his windpipe.

This person was good.

The Salarian staggered back and tried to bring up his pistol, but his opponent grabbed his hand almost casually. She twisted her body, spinning into him, and then rolled the alien over her hip and shoulder. He hit the ground hard with his back first, and his own gun was now pointed at his face.

A shot rang out, but somehow the Salarian had manifested a barrier at the last instance. In a move Jane had never seen before, he focussed the barrier into an outward blast that knocked the human backward and on her behind. The pistol went flying.

Both fighters were up in less than a second. They circled each other now, the Salarian panting and bleeding. The woman was just breathing a little heavier and her long, black hair had gotten somewhat messy; beyond that, there was no sign she had physically exerted herself at all. Her face exuded determination.

For a couple of seconds, nobody committed. The woman flinched and the Salarian took a step back, than he feinted right and she evaded; but no punches were thrown.

Then the little hearing Jane had left with all the blood rushing in her ears was blown away by the noise of an assault rifle being unloaded at full auto mere centimeters from her ear; Tali had retrieved the gun of the Batarian Shepard had killed. Jane groaned and weakly tried to cover it, but the barrage had already stopped, Tali jumping out of the way of a warp with a yelp.

Her interruption had been all that was needed. When Jane got her eyes back on the fight, it was all but decided. The woman had to have landed a good hit somewhere on the alien's body, because he was hunched over and his movements had slowed down. There was no more aggression from him, he could only defend himself from the lightning fast series of strikes and kicks coming his way.

The Salarian managed for all of four seconds. Then a heel connected to his temple and sent him into the dirt. To his credit, he immediately tried to get back up; but his opponent did not give him the opportunity. She finished it with a stamping kick on the alien's throat.

Jane relaxed and let her head sink back onto the ground. The wound in her right chest hurt bad now, and every breath was agony. Breathing also got more laborious with every time. She coughed, and blood came out with it. Her mind was working slowly, but she still understood what was happening; the shot hat hit her in the right lung, and now there was blood both inside the airways and in the pleural space – and air too, potentially. If she wasn't treated soon, her right lung would gradually collapse under the pressure to make way for the blood and air. Then her heart and the blood vessels would be pushed into the left pleural cavity; at some point, the two large veins would get kinked, blood supply to the heart would stop, and she would die.

She found that she didn't care as much as she would've thought. The bastard was dead, and Tali was safe. She could see Tali again now; the quarian was bending over her, their savior next to Tali. Shepard took the opportunity to muster the woman; she was of stunning beauty to the point that it seemed strange for a fighter of this caliber. She wasn't thin, but thinner than someone with this kind of strength should've been. Perhaps she used biotics to power herself up...

She scolded herself for wasting what might well be her last thoughts on such fruitless nonsense and rolled her head to the right to look at Tali. Her frantic activity had stopped, and she was simply looking down at Shepard now. Jane looked back and smiled lopsidedly. She tried to say something, but no words would come out. All she could do was to lift her right hand, and not even that went as she had intended; she had wanted to cup Tali's mask, but all she managed was to limply drop her arm into Tali's lap.

Then she felt a sting in her glute; shortly after, there was another one in her chest. The pain in her chest began to wane, and her world rapidly turned into a blur of deep purple, and then nothing.
